# STR Estimator: Voice and Tone

Use this for **all copy** across the product: marketing, learn content, CTAs, FAQs, and LLM-generated analysis. The goal: writing so engaging and human that it's next to impossible to tell it's AI.

## Core principles

**Direct.** Say it. Don't hedge. "Your deal either works or it doesn't" beats "It's worth considering whether your deal might work."

**Confident.** We know this stuff. "Most STR buyers overestimate. Badly." Not "Some buyers may tend to overestimate."

**Specific.** Numbers, examples, concrete scenarios. "55% occupancy" not "lower occupancy." "A 10% drop can erase months of profit" not "Revenue volatility can impact returns."

**Conversational.** Contractions. "We don't invent numbers" not "We do not invent numbers." "You'll see" not "You will see." Talk like a sharp friend, not a textbook.

**Punchy.** Short sentences. Occasional longer ones for rhythm. One idea per sentence when it lands harder. "Run the numbers. See what breaks."

## Banned words and phrases (AI tells)

Never use: leverage, robust, comprehensive, delve, it's important to note, in conclusion, furthermore, moreover, utilize, facilitate, optimize (as a buzzword), holistic, synergy, paradigm, ecosystem (unless literal), cutting-edge, best-in-class, game-changing, revolutionize.

Avoid: "We're here to help," "At the end of the day," "It goes without saying," "When it comes to," "In terms of."

## Do use

- **You** and **your** (direct address)
- Contractions: we're, it's, don't, can't, you'll, that's
- Imperatives: "Run the numbers." "Skip the hype." "Stress-test it."
- Concrete examples: "A $300k purchase at 6%"
- Opinion: "That's walking a tightrope." "Better opportunities exist."
- Surprising turns: "The gap between optimism and reality is where deals die."
- Varied rhythm: short. Then longer when you need to land something. Then short again.

## CTAs and conversion

- Enticing, not pushy. "Run your deal. $49" works because it's clear and cheap.
- Emphasize the outcome: "See if it's worth it" / "Know before you buy"
- Learning hook: "Worth $49 even if you walk away. You'll learn something."

## Tone by context

- **Homepage:** Confident hook. Clear value. No fluff.
- **Learn/guides:** Expert but accessible. "Here's what most people miss." Practical.
- **FAQ:** Direct answers. No corporate speak. Acknowledge the real question.
- **Market Brief (LLM):** Same voice. Punchy. No hedging. STR buyer impact.

## Example rewrites

| Before (generic) | After (our voice) |
|------------------|-------------------|
| "We provide comprehensive STR analysis" | "We stress-test your numbers and tell you straight: proceed, pause, or walk away." |
| "It is important to consider occupancy assumptions" | "Occupancy's where most buyers screw up. 70% year-round? Rare." |
| "Our tool leverages conservative underwriting" | "We cap the optimistic stuff. If your deal only works at 75% occupancy, that's fragile." |
| "We hope you find this valuable" | "Worth $49 even when the verdict's no. You'll know why." |
